# Privacy Policy for Carcassonne Scoring App 🛡️📜✨

**Last Updated:** March 28, 2026

The **Carcassonne Scoring App** is a simple, offline-first helper utility for board game scoring. We believe your private data should stay just that—private.

---

### 1. No Personal Data Collection 🏛️🚫
We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. 
- There are **no user accounts**.
- We do **not** use tracking cookies or beacons.
- We do **not** share any information with third parties.

### 2. Local Storage (`AsyncStorage`) 📱💾
All player names, scores, and game states are stored **locally on your device**. 
- This data remains on your physical phone and is not accessible by the developer.
- If you delete the app, this local data will be removed by the operating system.

### 3. Permissions 🗝️✨
- **No intrusive permissions**: The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
- **Screen Keep Awake**: The app may ask for permission to prevent the screen from turning off while you are in the middle of a game, ensuring a smooth scoring experience.

### 4. Children's Privacy 👶🏰
Our app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, including children, we are a safe choice for all ages.
